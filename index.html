A few useful things to know

Less guesswork. Clearer details.

How do I use this list?

Search or choose a filter, open the details on a card, and select Apply to visit the posting. Save interesting jobs to come back to them. No account is needed.

Are benefits and weekday hours guaranteed?

No. Labels reflect wording found in the posting. Check coverage, eligibility, employee contributions and waiting periods with the employer. Confirm whether evenings, weekends or overtime are required—even when a role mentions weekdays.

What does “posting page checked” mean?

A matching job page was successfully checked. It does not guarantee that the employer is still hiring or that an applicant meets every requirement. Always read the original posting.

Where are my saved jobs stored?

Only in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded or shared, and they do not sync between devices. Clearing browser data may remove them. Closed or removed postings may stop appearing in the saved list.

What kinds of jobs are included?

Office administration, scheduling, workforce planning, operations, training and client services, including French/English roles. Banking, insurance, contract and other fallback options are labelled separately in the details. Nearby locations and remote eligibility require individual review.

How often is the list updated?

Updates are scheduled daily. Job-board coverage is incomplete and updates can be delayed. The date at the top shows the latest discovery evidence; each card also shows its own posting and check dates.
